## Problem (2-3 sentences)

Curious urban dwellers, hunters, hikers, and pet owners encounter animal (and human) waste constantly but have no easy way to identify it or act on what it tells them. Hunters waste time tracking game blindly, pet owners can't tell if their dog's stool signals a health problem, and wildlife enthusiasts have no tool to know what animals live around them. Conservation researchers also lack a crowdsourced, accessible way to flag scat from rare or potentially returning species.

---

## Desired Outcome (2 sentences)

Users can snap a photo of any scat and instantly get a species ID, health or freshness insight, and a logged entry in their personal collection. A hunter knows if a deer passed through in the last hour, a pet owner knows if their cat needs more fiber, and a city kid discovers a fox lives two blocks away — all from one photo.
